## LIQUIDITY PLAN

**The standing rule: 25% of the total revenue from every Milky Way Galaxy
Collection release goes into $GALAXY liquidity.** Not just the first one — the same
share, every collection, so the pool grows as the galaxy does.

The galaxy pays for its own foundation. No outside capital, no borrowed hype.

### Why a pool needs depth
In a shallow pool, a small trade moves the price a long way. That is not a flaw in
the token — it is simple arithmetic, and it is the exact condition MEV bots look
for. Depth is the fix. The deeper the pool, the less a single trade can swing it,
and the more the price reflects real demand instead of noise.

### Why it is scheduled, not dumped
Putting the whole amount in at once produces one spike and then nothing. Spreading
it across four weeks gives steady support and keeps each step small enough to be
uninteresting to front-runners.

Each injection splits between two kinds. **Single-sided** additions put XRP in on
its own, which pushes the price up. **Balanced** additions go in on both sides,
which leaves the price where it is and makes the pool deeper. The schedule uses
both, weighted towards balanced.

Balanced additions need GALAXY as well as XRP, and the issuer wallet holds none by
design. That side is bought on the open market and goes straight into the pool.

### The schedule
| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 100 XRP | 60 balanced, 40 single-sided in six steps — **complete** |
| Week 2 | 50 XRP | single-sided in five steps — **complete** |
| Week 3 | 50 XRP | balanced |
| Week 4 | 50 XRP | 30 balanced, 20 single-sided in smaller steps |

Week 1 went in as planned — eleven transactions in total, all published.
Week 2 went in single-sided rather than balanced: five steps of 10 XRP each, taking
the pool from **858 to 908 XRP**.

The transaction hash is posted after every injection, so anyone can open it on an
explorer and see the amount, the direction and the date for themselves.

A further portion of each collection's revenue is set aside for what comes next —
artwork for the following planets, collaborations, the game, and the physical work.
